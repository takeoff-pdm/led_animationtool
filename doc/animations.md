## Strobe ⬜⬛⬜⬛
**Description**: *Flashes the color on the beat*

**Variations**:
1. *Flash on the beat and turn off after 1/3 of the beat*
2. *Flash twice per beat and each flash turns off after 1/6 of the beat*
3. *Flash three times per beat and each flash turns off after 1/8 of the beat*

**Directions** (Direction is used to determine how often a flash occurs):
1. Every beat
2. Every 2nd beat
3. Every 4th beat
4. Every 8th beat
